LEGAL INFORMATION

Privacy policy.

This initial notice explains how the current public website handles information. It must be reviewed against the company’s actual practices before formal use.

Review note: This is an initial website notice, not legal advice. StarnyX Technologies should review it against its actual business practices and applicable requirements before relying on it as a formal legal policy.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Information you submit

The public enquiry forms ask for your name, an optional WhatsApp number, the service you are interested in, an optional budget range and project details. Do not submit passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information through these forms.

WhatsApp enquiry handoff

When you submit an enquiry form, the website prepares a message and opens WhatsApp. You decide whether to send that message by tapping Send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp processes messages under its own terms and privacy practices.

Private Discord notification

When the website's Discord notification is configured, the same enquiry details are also sent through a server-side Vercel function to a private StarnyX Discord channel for internal follow-up. The Discord webhook secret is kept in the hosting environment and is not included in the public website code. Discord processes the notification under its own terms and privacy practices.

Website technology

This public website uses HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It does not currently provide user accounts or online payment collection. Web fonts may be requested from Google Fonts, and your browser/network provider may process technical information when external resources are loaded.

Storage and retention

The public website does not include a customer database in this phase. Enquiry details may be present in the WhatsApp conversation you choose to send and in the private Discord notification when configured. Retention and deletion practices for those services should be reviewed by the account administrators.
