policy.

How we handle information shared through this website.

LAST REVIEWED7 NOV 2026

General website information. Project-specific written terms take precedence where applicable.

01

Information you submit

When you use an enquiry form, the information you choose to provide may include your name, WhatsApp number, requested service, indicative budget and project description.

02

WhatsApp enquiry handoff

When you submit a form, your browser prepares a message and opens WhatsApp. You review the message and decide whether to send it. WhatsApp then handles the message under its own terms and privacy practices.

03

Private notification

If the website notification service is configured, enquiry details may also be sent to a private StarnyX Discord channel through a server-side integration. The webhook address is not exposed in the browser. If that service is not configured, WhatsApp remains the enquiry path.

04

Website technology

The site may use essential browser storage or basic technical logs required for security, performance and navigation. Third-party font resources may be requested from Google Fonts.

05

Storage and retention

We retain enquiry information only as reasonably needed to respond, discuss a potential project, maintain business records or meet applicable obligations. Do not submit sensitive personal information through this website.

06

Your choices

You may choose not to submit an enquiry. You can contact us through our public WhatsApp channel with a request about information you previously shared.
