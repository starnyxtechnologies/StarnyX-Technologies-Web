What kind of projects does StarnyX work on?+

We work on websites, web applications, mobile applications, UI/UX design, cloud foundations and practical automation or integrations.

How does a project usually begin?+

We begin with a conversation about your goal, audience, current situation and constraints. When the brief is clear, we outline a suitable scope, approach and next steps.

Can I request a custom quotation?+

Yes. Share the features you need, any reference links, your indicative budget and preferred timeline. We will review the details and respond with the next step.

Do you work with existing websites or products?+

Yes. We can discuss improvements, redesigns, additional features, integrations or technical reviews of an existing product.

How long does a website or application take?+

Timing depends on the requirements, content readiness, integrations and review cycles. We agree milestones after the scope is understood.

Do you provide fixed package prices?+

We prefer requirement-based estimates. A written proposal explains deliverables, assumptions and costs before the project begins.

How do we communicate during a project?+

Communication channels, review points and response expectations are agreed during project setup. WhatsApp is the public enquiry channel on this website.

Do you provide hosting and maintenance?+

We can discuss deployment, hosting configuration and ongoing support as part of the project scope. Third-party account and subscription costs remain separate unless explicitly included.
