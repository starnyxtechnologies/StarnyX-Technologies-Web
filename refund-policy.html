How project payments and cancellations are handled.

LAST REVIEWED7 NOV 2026

General website information. Project-specific written terms take precedence where applicable.

01

Current website status

This is a public marketing and enquiry website. It does not sell subscriptions, downloadable products or services through an online checkout.

02

Project engagements

If you enter into a project with StarnyX, payment milestones, cancellation rights, refunds and any non-refundable costs will be stated in the project-specific written proposal or agreement.

03

Third-party costs

Hosting, domains, software licences, paid APIs and other third-party charges may be billed by the relevant provider. Their refund rules are controlled by that provider unless a written agreement states otherwise.

04

Before making a payment

Review the scope, deliverables, milestones, assumptions and cancellation terms in writing. Do not make a payment based only on an informal message or website description.

05

Questions

For clarification about a specific proposal, contact StarnyX through WhatsApp before making payment.
