AGREEMENT / PROJECT DELIVERY

Project service agreement

How project-specific terms should be documented.

LAST REVIEWED7 NOV 2026

General website information. Project-specific written terms take precedence where applicable.

01

Project-specific agreement

A project begins only after the parties agree the scope, deliverables, milestones, fees, payment schedule and other material terms in writing.

02

Items to define

A written proposal or agreement should identify responsibilities, content and access supplied by the client, review and acceptance steps, change requests, third-party costs, support arrangements and any applicable cancellation terms.

03

Changes to scope

Requests outside the agreed scope may affect price and schedule. They should be assessed and approved in writing before the additional work begins.

04

Acceptance

The parties should agree how deliverables are reviewed, what constitutes acceptance and how defects or requested changes are handled.

05

Contact

This page is a general outline, not a signed contract or legal advice. The final project-specific agreement governs the engagement.
