Projects & services

What services do you offer?

Our service areas include web development, mobile applications, UI/UX design, cloud and DevOps foundations, and workflow automation or integrations.

Can you help improve an existing website or application?

Yes. Share the current setup, the issues you want to solve and your goals. We can discuss whether an improvement, redesign or technical update makes sense.

Do you take custom projects?

Yes. We review the requirements and determine a suitable scope, deliverables and approach before providing a proposal.

Process & pricing

How does a project begin?

It begins with an enquiry and a discovery conversation about goals, audience, requirements, timing and constraints. A written scope and quotation can follow.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on functionality, complexity, design needs, integrations and delivery scope. We provide a project-specific quotation after understanding the requirements.

How long will a project take?

Timing depends on scope, feedback cycles, content and external dependencies. A proposed schedule is discussed as part of the quotation.

Communication & delivery

How can I contact your team?

Will I receive project updates?

Communication and review checkpoints are agreed for each engagement so that progress, questions and decisions remain visible.

Can I request ongoing support after launch?

Support and maintenance can be discussed separately and documented in the project proposal or support agreement.
